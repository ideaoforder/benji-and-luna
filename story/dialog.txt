# Benji & Luna: everything the characters say.
#
# Fill this in! Change any line, add more lines, or delete one. The game reads this file as it is.
#
# HOW IT WORKS
#   [section]          where in the game the lines are said (the list of sections is below; keep the names as they are)
#   Speaker: words     one line. Keep it short: it has to fit in a box, and someone might be reading it out loud.
#   # anything         a note for you; the game skips it
#
# STORY sections (area, boss, beaten, ride, ending...) pause the game and show a box at the bottom, one line at a
# time; any button goes to the next line. Each is shown once per campaign (a fresh campaign shows them again).
# BARK sections (bark ...) are a quick bubble over someone's head in the middle of play. The game picks ONE line at
# random each time, so put in as many different ones as you like.
#
# SPEAKERS
#   Benji, Luna, Luca, Cricket, Simon   that cat, if someone is playing it (if not, whichever cat is out)
#   Cat                                 whichever cat player 1 is playing right now
#   Friend                              another cat: player 2's, or player 1's partner
#   Chief, Warden, Trapper, Shadow Master, Drill Captain, Director, Helicopter, Commissioner   the bosses
#   Scrappy, Maneki                     the shopkeepers
#   Officer                             an Animal Control officer on screen (barks only)
#   Narrator                            nobody: just words


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the areas: when the cats first arrive

[area streets]
Benji: Animal Control took our friends to the pound!
Luna: Then we go and get them. Follow me!

[area castle]
Luca: A castle? Who keeps cats in a castle?
Benji: The Warden does. Let's knock.

[area jungle]
Simon: Ooh, jungle! Stay close, everyone. I brought a compass.
Luna: It's pointing at a snack.

[area sakura]
Cricket: Cherry blossoms! This is the most beautiful place I have ever been.
Benji: Watch out for ninjas.

[area caverns]
Luna: It's dark down here...
Simon: Crystals give off light. Mostly.

[area lab]
Luca: It's so cold my whiskers are frozen.
Benji: The Director is in there somewhere. Let's go!

[area airship]
Narrator: The Director's last snowcat leads to a giant Animal Control airship.
Luna: All aboard!

[area tower]
Benji: Animal Control Headquarters.
Luna: The only way out is down!


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the bosses: when they walk in

[boss chief]
Chief: So you're the cats who keep getting away.
Benji: And we're taking our friends with us!

[boss warden]
Warden: Nobody escapes my castle!
Luca: Watch us.

[boss trapper]
Trapper: Nets, nets, and more nets. You'll never get past them all!
Simon: Challenge accepted.

[boss shadow master]
Shadow Master: You cannot see me... but I can see you.
Cricket: I can hear you, though. Very loud whispering.

[boss drill captain]
Drill Captain: You're in my mine now, kitties!
Benji: Not for long!

[boss director]
Director: My experiments will NOT be interrupted.
Luna: Too late!

[boss helicopter]
Helicopter: Attention, cats. Land your boards and come quietly.
Cat: Never!

[boss commissioner]
Commissioner: I am the head of all of Animal Control. This ends now.
Benji: You're right. It does.


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the bosses: when they are beaten

[beaten chief]
Chief: This isn't over!
Luna: Yes it is. Let's go, everybody!

[beaten warden]
Warden: My castle...

[beaten trapper]
Trapper: Tangled... in my own net...

[beaten shadow master]
Shadow Master: Impressive, little paws.

[beaten drill captain]
Drill Captain: My beautiful drill!

[beaten director]
Director: But... my research...
Luca: Your research needs more cats being free.

[beaten helicopter]

[ending]
Commissioner: Fine! Fine! Animal Control will leave the cats alone!
Benji: And every cat in the pound goes home.
Luna: Who wants to go home?
Narrator: The end. Thanks for playing!
