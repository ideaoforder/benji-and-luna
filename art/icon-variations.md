# Icon style variations for family review

Requested alternatives to the current icon set: keep the liked yarn balls and hearts, explore a feel closer to the original, plus one very different option. Interpreted original as the earlier emoji presentation; no exact platform emoji screenshot was supplied.

- A: `art/raw/concept-icons-a-soft-emoji.png` — smooth glossy emoji volume, plush yarn, candy hearts.
- B: `art/raw/concept-icons-b-rounded-cartoon.png` — rounded illustrated shapes, lighter colored outlines, simple shading; a bridge between emoji and the game artwork.
- C: `art/raw/concept-icons-c-paper-stickers.png` — matte cut-paper shapes, bold white sticker borders, a contrasting visual direction.

All three retain all12 icons in the existing order. The cream and petrol-blue backgrounds are presentation backgrounds, not transparency. These are concept alternatives pending preference feedback; they do not replace art/icons.png or any live assets. Production versions should use a removable key or transparent background and be tested at32px before adoption.

All originals preserved byte-for-byte; hashes in icon-variations-originals.json. Visually checked counts, order and complete silhouettes. No runtime files changed.
