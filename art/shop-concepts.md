# Shop visual concepts

User request: tool shop run by Scrappy the Capy (junkyard capybara), specials dojo run by a waving neko cat.

- `art/raw/concept-shop-scrappy.png`: warm orange shipping-container workshop, salvaged teal car-front counter, sleepy friendly capybara with work apron, blue bandana and welding goggles. Tools displayed as repaired junkyard inventions and colorful yarn ammunition.
- `art/raw/concept-shop-dojo.png`: working name Lucky Paw Dojo, cream/red/jade timber interior, living calico maneki-neko with beckoning paw, gold bell and black belt. Scrolls and three move emblems make learning specials visually distinct from buying tools.

These are visual ideas, not drop-in room backgrounds or final character sheets. Shopkeepers, counter, stock, signage and effects are baked together. Do not auto-import as scenery.

Suggested animation: Scrappy blinks slowly, adjusts goggles, presents a repaired tool; dojo master gently beckons, bows, then demonstrates a paw gesture. For production, create separate empty backgrounds, merchant sprites, counter foregrounds and merchandise/move icons. Keep prices and descriptions in UI, not painted signage. Verify room framing and approach space against the eventual shop implementation before drawing production layers. No new mechanics or shop prices are prescribed here.

Both saved PNGs are untouched generator outputs. Hashes and source filenames are recorded in shop-concepts-originals.json. No runtime files changed.
